# Remove and heal

## Remove

Press `Q`, then paint over what should go: a person, a sign, a wire, dust. When you let go, a local inpainting model fills the area from its surroundings. A click without dragging removes the object under the pointer.

`[` and `]` (or the scroll wheel) change the brush size.

Each removal is listed in the Remove & Heal section and can be restored with its ×; undo works as for any other change. The original file is never altered.

## Heal and clone

Press `H`, then drag from the blemish to a clean area: the blemish is replaced by that area, matched in brightness (heal) or copied as is (clone). Drag either circle to move it, scroll to resize, `⌫` deletes the selected spot.
