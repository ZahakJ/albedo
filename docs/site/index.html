A RAW developer and compositor that runs entirely in your browser.

Develop on the GPU, then work in layers with the RAW still inside. The AI runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

Open albedo Manual Source

Tour

Develop

Every slider in the next frame

  • The whole edit runs on the GPU: 2.4 ms a frame on 24 MP, 2.3 ms on 45 MP with eight masks.
  • Masks for subject, sky, background or any object you click. The subject takes 93 ms.
  • Remove, heal and AI Denoise on the RAW data, 45 MP in 3.8 s.
  • RCD and X-Trans demosaicing, highlight rebuilding, lensfun corrections, AgX or DCP profiles.

Compose

Layers, with the RAW still inside

  • A photo arrives from Develop as a smart object: change its development at any time.
  • Develop and Photoshop-style adjustment layers, masks, Blend If, 27 blend modes, groups, styles, 8, 16 or 32 bits.
  • AI selection, refine edge, content-aware fill, heal, clone, liquify, warp, brushes with pen pressure.
  • A 50-layer, 24 MP document redraws in 6.7 ms.

Type and files

Arabic sets right, files go anywhere

  • Type shaped by HarfBuzz: Arabic joins and mixed-direction lines with no setting to switch.
  • PSD in and out with layers, masks and modes. Layered TIFF in.
  • Lightroom XMP sidecars and presets, read and written. DNG and 16-bit TIFF out.
  • A Library to cull from the keyboard: 10,000 photos filter in 12–19 ms.
