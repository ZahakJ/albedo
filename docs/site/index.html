Tour

Compose

Layers, with the RAW still inside

  • A photo comes from Develop as a smart object: change its development any time, with no file in between.
  • Every Develop control as an adjustment layer, plus Curves, Levels, Hue/Saturation and the rest, through masks and Blend If.
  • All 27 Photoshop blend modes, clipping, groups, layer styles, 8, 16 and 32 bits.
  • Select subject, sky or an object with local AI; refine the edge for hair; content-aware fill with no credits.
  • Heal, clone, dodge and burn, frequency separation, liquify, warp: pen pressure throughout.

Type and files

Arabic sets right the first time

  • Type shaped by HarfBuzz: Arabic joins, ligatures and mixed right-to-left lines, with no setting to switch.
  • Your installed fonts, or any font file; kerning, tracking, box text.
  • PSD in and out at 8, 16 and 32 bits with layers, groups, masks and modes; layered TIFF in.
  • A 50-layer, 24-megapixel document redraws in 7 ms: it is drawn at the size of your screen.

Library

Cull from the keyboard

  • P X U flag, 0–5 rate, 6–9 label.
  • Full-screen cull, compare and survey; the next photos are ready before you get there.
  • Albums, smart collections, keywords, a capture-date tree.
  • 10,000 photos filter in about 15 ms.

Local AI

Masks, Remove and Denoise, on your GPU

  • Subject, sky, background, or any object from a click or a box.
  • Remove: paint over a distraction, or click it, and it is filled from its surroundings.
  • AI Denoise works on the RAW data; 45 megapixels in about four seconds.
  • No account, no credits, no upload.

Develop

Every slider in the next frame

  • The whole edit runs on the GPU: about 2 ms a frame on a 61-megapixel file.
  • RCD demosaicing, a GPU X-Trans path, highlights rebuilt instead of turning magenta.
  • An AgX look by default, or Adobe and RawTherapee camera profiles.
  • Lens corrections from the lensfun database, applied on open.

No lock-in

Your files, your edits, anywhere

  • Lightroom and Camera Raw .xmp sidecars and presets, read and written.
  • Export the original with a sidecar, a DNG with the edit inside, or a 16-bit TIFF.
  • Installs as an app, works offline, opens RAW files from the desktop.
  • Free software under the AGPL.
